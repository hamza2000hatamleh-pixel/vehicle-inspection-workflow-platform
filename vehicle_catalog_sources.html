Vehicle catalog sources

The dropdowns use an adapted snapshot of MeterApp Vehicle DB, version 2.67.0. The app retains make, model and observed year, groups duplicate model-year observations, and supplements them with workshop records.

Coverage varies by market. Some sources use manufacture or registration year. Confirm the model year with the vehicle documents.
